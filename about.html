About CyberSafe

CyberSafe: A Web-Based Cybersecurity Awareness System is an educational website designed to help internet users become more aware of cybersecurity threats and safe online practices. It explains common risks in simple language and offers practical steps you can use every day.

Professional Ethics Principles

Our project applies these principles to support respectful and responsible technology use.

Privacy and Confidentiality

Lessons need no personal details, and quiz responses stay in your browser.

Accuracy and Honesty

Guidance uses credible sources. Fictional examples are labeled, and no protection is described as a guarantee.

Intellectual Property Respect

We acknowledge information sources and use original, locally stored icons instead of copying others’ artwork.

Accessibility and Inclusivity

Readable text, keyboard controls, visible focus, and responsive layouts help more people use the website.

Responsibility and Public Safety

Lessons encourage prevention, reporting, and seeking support without promoting harmful behavior.

Integrity

The quiz reports scores honestly, explains answers, and does not request hidden permissions or collect responses.

Ethical guidance: Association for Computing Machinery (ACM) — Code of Ethics and Professional Conduct (PDF).

Privacy and Security Considerations

  • CyberSafe does not require login or registration.
  • No personal information is required to access lessons.
  • Quiz answers are processed locally in your browser and are not transmitted to a server.
  • Answers are held only in memory. Refreshing or closing the page clears them.
  • The website uses no tracking scripts, cookies, or unnecessary data collection.
  • Reference links lead to external websites, which have their own privacy policies.

Our privacy approach is informed by the Philippine Data Privacy Act of 2012, including transparency, legitimate purpose, and proportionality. CyberSafe avoids requesting personal information for lessons and quizzes.

Data privacy reference: National Privacy Commission (Philippines) — Republic Act No. 10173 — Data Privacy Act of 2012.
